## Who operates Pixinium

Pixinium is operated by Tanuki in Vietnam. Please use the relevant email below so your request can be identified correctly. Include only information needed to understand and resolve the issue.

## Account and website support

Email [support@pixinium.com](mailto:support@pixinium.com) for account access, downloads, broken links, usage-rights questions or website issues. Include the page URL, a short description, the time of the issue and your browser/device where relevant. Do not send your password or an active recovery link.

## Privacy and account deletion

Email [privacy@pixinium.com](mailto:privacy@pixinium.com) for access, correction, deletion and other personal-data requests. Where possible, write from the email linked to your account and explain which information or account the request concerns. We may ask for proportionate verification before changing or disclosing account information.

Deleting browser cookies or signing out does not delete your account. Account deletion is currently handled by request, rather than an automatic in-site delete button. Read the [Privacy Policy](/policies/privacy) for retention and any lawful exceptions to deletion.

## Copyright and removal reviews

Email [copyright@pixinium.com](mailto:copyright@pixinium.com). Include the work, affected Pixinium URLs, your relationship to the rights holder, the reason for the complaint and relevant evidence. The [Copyright & Takedown Policy](/policies/copyright) explains the information to provide and how to ask for a review of a mistaken removal.

## Abuse and security concerns

Email [support@pixinium.com](mailto:support@pixinium.com) with the affected URL and a safe description of the concern. For a security issue, describe the impact and minimal steps needed to understand it. Do not exploit access to other users' data, perform disruptive tests or attach suspected malware. We do not promise a bounty or a fixed response time.

## What happens next

We review requests, may ask for clarification and respond according to the issue and applicable legal deadlines. Keep any reference included in our reply. If you disagree with an account or content decision, explain why and include supporting information so we can review it.
