## Check each item's permissions

Pixinium is an archive, not a single license covering every file. Download availability, a category label or a "free" description does not establish permission for commercial use, redistribution or modification. Check the item's description, file documentation, creator source and any attached license before using it.

If the item does not state a license or the information conflicts, do not assume additional rights. Contact [support@pixinium.com](mailto:support@pixinium.com) or the rights holder for clarification. Rights and exceptions provided directly by applicable law are not removed by this page.

## Personal and commercial use

Personal use and commercial use can have different conditions. A permission to use an image as a personal wallpaper does not necessarily allow using it in branding, advertising, merchandise or a client project. Confirm the intended use is covered by the applicable permission.

## Modification, sharing and resale

- Check whether cropping, recoloring, remixing, editing audio or other modifications are allowed.
- Check whether redistribution of original files, modified files or source assets is permitted.
- Do not sell or repackage a file unless the relevant rights expressly allow that use.
- Do not claim a creator's work as your own or remove required copyright and license notices.
- Where credit is required, follow the license's stated attribution format and preserve links or notices it requires.

## Games, music and asset packs

Different parts of a download may carry separate terms. A game's executable, soundtrack, fonts, characters and bundled assets may not share one license. A license to listen to music does not necessarily permit synchronization in videos, streaming, redistribution or use in a game. Check included license and readme files as well as the item page.

## Platform and creator rights

Creators and other rights holders retain their rights. Pixinium can only grant permissions it has authority to grant. Site terms govern use of the platform and do not override an item's valid third-party license. When an item is removed, any independent license you hold continues or ends according to that license's own terms.

## Unclear or incorrect information

Ask support to clarify missing or inconsistent permissions. Include the item URL and your intended use. If you believe a file is distributed without authorization, use the [Copyright & Takedown Policy](/policies/copyright).
