## Who these rules apply to

These rules apply to browsing, accounts, profiles, downloads, support requests and authorized publishing or API access. Pixinium is a curated archive. Uploading and publishing catalog items are currently restricted to authorized administrators; membership does not give general upload privileges.

## Prohibited content and behavior

- Illegal content or conduct, and material that violates another person's copyright, privacy or other rights.
- Files you are not entitled to distribute, misleading licenses or removal of required creator attribution.
- Malware, credential stealers, destructive code or files designed to compromise a device, account or network.
- Spam, scams, phishing, fraudulent claims and impersonation of people, creators or the Pixinium team.
- Threats, targeted harassment, exploitative content or disclosure of another person's private information without a lawful basis.
- Deceptive file names, descriptions or download links intended to conceal harmful or unauthorized material.

## Accounts, APIs and automation

Do not access another person's account, bypass authorization, evade a suspension, probe for secrets or intentionally disrupt availability. Do not overwhelm authentication, search, media delivery or download endpoints with abusive automated traffic.

Administrator and agent-upload credentials must stay private and be used only within their granted permissions. Do not use automation to publish unreviewed or unauthorized content, evade size or access limits, or distribute credentials. Contact us before running bulk operations that could materially affect service availability.

## Publishing responsibilities

Authorized publishers must confirm distribution rights, describe files accurately, preserve required attribution and avoid misleading safety or compatibility claims. A successful upload is not evidence of a valid license or a guarantee that the file is safe. Correct mistakes promptly and cooperate with rights and security reviews.

## Reporting a concern

Email [support@pixinium.com](mailto:support@pixinium.com) with the affected URL, a description of the issue, relevant timestamps and evidence you can safely share. For copyright complaints, use [Copyright & Takedown](/policies/copyright). For personal-data concerns, contact [privacy@pixinium.com](mailto:privacy@pixinium.com).

Do not attach suspected malware, passwords, reset links or unrelated private information. Describe how to identify the file or issue instead.

## Review and enforcement

We assess reports and may request clarification, restrict access, remove content or suspend publishing or account privileges according to severity, available evidence and applicable law. Urgent risks may require action before a full review. We may preserve relevant evidence or refer a matter to competent authorities when appropriate and lawful.

If you believe an action was mistaken, contact support with the affected URL or account reference and supporting information. We will consider the response. Reports are reviewed rather than treated as proof by themselves.
