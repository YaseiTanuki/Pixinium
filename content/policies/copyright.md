## Respect for creators

Pixinium respects the rights of creators and other rights holders. A file being publicly accessible elsewhere does not establish permission to distribute it. Our [Asset Usage Rights](/policies/usage-rights) explain how individual permissions apply to downloads.

## Where to send a copyright report

Send reports to [copyright@pixinium.com](mailto:copyright@pixinium.com). Use a subject such as "Copyright report — item title". You should be the rights holder or authorized to act on their behalf. This is a general copyright reporting channel, not a representation that Pixinium has a registered statutory takedown agent in every country.

## Information to include

- Your name, a working contact email and your relationship to the rights holder.
- Identification of the protected work, with an original source or other supporting evidence where available.
- The exact Pixinium URLs and the specific files or parts you believe infringe your rights.
- An explanation of the rights involved and why the distribution or use is unauthorized.
- A statement that you believe the report is accurate and that you are the rights holder or authorized representative.
- Your typed name or signature, and any additional information legally required for the procedure you are invoking.

Provide enough detail to assess the claim, but avoid unnecessary sensitive information. A general allegation without an identifiable item may require clarification before we can act.

## How we review reports

We review the information, may ask for clarification and assess available permissions, source material and applicable law. We may temporarily restrict access while investigating. Depending on the findings, we may remove or restrict the content, correct attribution or license information, or decline an unsupported request.

Where lawful and appropriate, we notify the affected contributor or administrator and explain how they can respond. We share only information reasonably needed for the process. Submitting a report does not guarantee automatic removal, a fixed outcome or an immediate response.

## If content was removed by mistake

Reply to the notice or email [copyright@pixinium.com](mailto:copyright@pixinium.com) with the affected URL, any reference we supplied, your contact details and why you believe removal was mistaken. Include relevant licenses, permissions, authorship evidence or a legal basis for the use.

We will review the response and may contact the reporting party. We may restore content if the issue is resolved and restoration is lawful. Any formal statutory notice, counter-notice or court process is handled under the applicable law; this policy does not promise a universal restoration deadline.

## Repeated violations and misuse of reports

We may restrict publishing privileges or suspend or terminate accounts involved in repeated or serious copyright violations, taking account of the evidence, responses and applicable law. Do not submit knowingly false or abusive complaints. You may ask us to review a restriction through the same contact channel.
