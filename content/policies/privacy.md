## Who is responsible

Tanuki, based in Vietnam, operates Pixinium and is responsible for the personal information described here. Send privacy questions and requests to [privacy@pixinium.com](mailto:privacy@pixinium.com). This policy explains our account, archive, support and website operations; external services you visit have their own policies.

## Information we use

- Account information: email address, authentication user ID, username, account role, account timestamps and an avatar you upload. When you choose Google sign-in, the authentication provider may supply profile information such as your name and avatar.
- Account activity: items saved to your collection, profile changes and actions you take through authorized account features.
- Files and content: avatar uploads and, for authorized administrators, uploaded assets, descriptions and publishing activity.
- Technical information: request times, IP addresses and browser or device information that infrastructure providers may process in connection with delivery, security and logs.
- Usage information: page views, referrers, approximate region and device/browser categories through web analytics when enabled.
- Correspondence: your email, the details you provide and relevant evidence when you contact us or submit a rights or abuse report.

Passwords and authentication credentials are handled by Supabase Auth. Pixinium's application database does not store a copy of your password. Avoid including passwords, reset links or unnecessary sensitive information in correspondence.

## Why we use information

We use information to create and secure accounts, authenticate sessions, display profiles, keep saved collections, deliver files and transactional emails, investigate reports, prevent abuse, maintain the service and understand how the archive is used.

We process information to provide services you request, meet applicable legal obligations and pursue permitted service and security purposes. Where the law requires your consent, we must obtain it for the relevant processing; accepting website terms does not replace a separate consent requirement. You can withdraw consent where applicable without affecting processing that was lawful before withdrawal.

## Service providers and disclosures

Providers receive information needed for their role. The services used by this website include:

- Supabase: account authentication, identity-provider connections, sessions and recovery flows.
- Neon: application account records, profiles, saved collections and catalog data. The configured object-storage service stores uploaded files such as avatars and catalog assets.
- Vercel Web Analytics: website usage statistics in production when analytics is enabled. It uses request-derived visitor identifiers rather than third-party analytics cookies. See [Vercel's analytics privacy information](https://vercel.com/docs/analytics/privacy-policy).
- Cloudflare, where configured for the deployment: domain delivery, security, caching or storage services may process network requests and related logs.
- Brevo, where configured as the email delivery provider: recipient addresses, transactional email content and delivery diagnostics needed to send account messages.
- Hosting and email providers: technical processing and correspondence needed to run the website and respond to requests.

We may disclose relevant information when required by law, to respond to a valid legal request or to protect people, accounts and rights. Information needed to assess a complaint may be shared with an affected party where necessary and lawful; we do not automatically publish private contact details. We do not sell your personal information.

## Storage, security and international processing

We use access controls, server-side authorization and protected connections to reduce unauthorized access. Administrator privileges are separate from ordinary account access. No system is completely secure; report suspected account compromise to support.

Our providers may process information outside Vietnam. Applicable destinations and safeguards depend on the providers and deployment configuration. You can contact us for information about processing locations and applicable transfer safeguards. We must comply with the legal requirements that apply to those transfers.

## Retention

We retain account and saved-collection information while needed to provide your account. When you request deletion, we assess what can be deleted and what must be retained for a specific legal, security or dispute-resolution purpose. We do not treat an unused account as an automatic deletion request.

Technical logs and analytics are retained according to their operational purpose and the configured provider retention settings. Correspondence and report evidence are kept while needed to resolve the matter and meet applicable obligations. Backup copies may remain until the relevant backup cycle expires; they are not intended for routine use after deletion. Ask us for the retention information relevant to your request rather than assuming every category has the same period.

## Your choices and rights

You can change available profile details through your account and remove items from your saved collection. For information that cannot be changed in the interface, contact [privacy@pixinium.com](mailto:privacy@pixinium.com).

Depending on applicable law, you may request access, correction, a copy, deletion or restriction of processing; object to processing; withdraw consent; or raise a complaint with a competent authority. We may need proportionate identity verification. Do not send a password or identity document unless we explain why a specific verification step is necessary. We will respond within the period required by applicable law and explain any lawful limitation on a request.

Read the [Cookie Policy](/policies/cookies) for session storage and browser choices. Do not submit a child's information without the authorization required by applicable law. If you believe a child has provided information improperly, contact us so we can investigate and take appropriate action.

Material changes to data practices will be reflected here with an updated date and appropriate notice. New purposes requiring consent will require that consent before processing begins.
