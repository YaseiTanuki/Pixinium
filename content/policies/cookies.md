## Cookies and similar storage

Cookies are small values your browser stores for a website. Browser storage and request-based identifiers can also support website features or measurement without being cookies. This policy covers these technologies alongside the [Privacy Policy](/policies/privacy).

## Essential and authentication cookies

Pixinium uses Supabase authentication cookies to maintain and refresh a signed-in session. Temporary recovery or sign-in cookies can connect an authentication request with its callback. Their names generally begin with "sb-" and depend on the configured Supabase project.

These cookies support the sign-in service you request. Removing or blocking them can sign you out or prevent login, email verification or password recovery from completing. Session lifetime is governed by the authentication configuration and refresh behavior; it is not a promise that a session remains valid until a browser cookie expires.

The hosting or security provider may also use necessary security or delivery technologies where configured, for example to mitigate abusive traffic. Such technologies should be used only for their stated operational purpose.

## Analytics

The production website includes Vercel Web Analytics when enabled. It measures visits and broad usage information, such as viewed pages, referrers, approximate region and browser/device categories. Vercel describes its visitor measurement as request-derived and without third-party cookies; this still involves processing usage data. See [Vercel's analytics privacy information](https://vercel.com/docs/analytics/privacy-policy).

An absence of analytics cookies does not by itself determine whether consent or another legal requirement applies in your location. Where consent is required, optional processing must wait for that consent.

## Advertising and affiliate tracking

This release does not integrate advertising cookies or affiliate tracking. A link to another website does not mean that Pixinium controls the storage used by that website. If advertising or affiliate tracking is introduced, this policy and any required choice controls must be updated before that tracking is activated.

## Managing your choices

You can view, block or delete cookies and site data in your browser settings. Most browsers also let you restrict third-party storage. Clearing site data does not delete your Pixinium account or saved collection, which are stored on the server.

Pixinium does not currently offer an in-site analytics preference switch. Browser privacy tools may block analytics requests. To ask about processing or exercise applicable privacy rights, contact [privacy@pixinium.com](mailto:privacy@pixinium.com). Blocking essential authentication storage can affect account features; you can still browse publicly available content.

We will update this page when relevant technologies or purposes change. Contact [privacy@pixinium.com](mailto:privacy@pixinium.com) with questions about browser storage or analytics on Pixinium.
